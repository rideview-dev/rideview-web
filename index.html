How It Works

How It Works.

Ride View connects brands with passengers in real time through a simple, powerful process.

  1. 1

    Tablets in Ride Vehicles

    High-quality screens installed on headrests across partner Uber and Lyft vehicles.

  2. 2

    Ads Shown During Trips

    Dynamic ads are displayed to engaged passengers during their entire ride.

  3. 3

    Exposure Tracked in Real-Time
